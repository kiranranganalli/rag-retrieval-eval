1. Business Overview
Copperleaf Kitchen & Roastery is an independent farm-to-table cafe, scratch bakery, and small-batch
coffee roastery operating two retail locations and one production warehouse in Brantley Heights and
Riverbend, Oregon. The business serves breakfast, lunch, and dinner, runs a wholesale coffee program,
and operates a full-service catering and private events arm.

1.2 Mission and Positioning
Copperleaf positions itself in the upper-middle casual segment. The average lunch ticket is $21.40 per
guest and the average dinner ticket is $38.60 per guest, which places the business above fast-casual
competitors and below the fine dining rooms downtown. The stated mission is to serve food built from
ingredients sourced within 120 miles of the kitchen, roasted and baked on premises, at a price a neighbor
can afford twice a week.

- A signed contract and a deposit of 25 percent of the estimated total confirm the date. The date is not
held without both.
- Final guest count is due 7 calendar days before the event. The count may rise after that but may not
fall; clients are billed on the higher of the final count or the actual attendance.
- Cancellation 30 or more days out: the deposit is fully refunded, less a $75 administrative fee.
- Cancellation 15 to 29 days out: 50 percent of the deposit is refunded.
- Cancellation 8 to 14 days out: the deposit is forfeited.
- Cancellation 7 days or fewer: the full estimated total is charged.
- A 20 percent service charge applies to all catering and private events. This is not a gratuity and is
distributed across the event team per the tip policy in section 9.4.
- Oregon has no state sales tax, so no sales tax is added. Corporate clients with approved credit may
request Net 30 billing in place of a card on file.

8.4 Pay, Benefits, and Scheduling Rules
 - Starting wage for baristas and line cooks is $19.50 per hour. Shift leads start at $23.00 and sous chefs
at $27.50.
- Schedules are posted 14 days in advance in Wheelhouse Scheduling. Shift swaps require manager
approval at least 24 hours before the shift.
- Employees who work 30 or more hours per week for two consecutive months become eligible for health
coverage, with the company paying 70 percent of the employee premium.
- Paid time off accrues at one hour for every 26 hours worked, capped at 96 hours per year.
- All staff receive a 50 percent discount on food at both cafes, and one free 12 ounce bag of coffee each
week.
- Anyone who has worked at least 90 days may request a transfer between sites; transfers are granted
based on business need and seniority.

9. Customer Policies
9.1 Reservations and Seating
 - Reservations are accepted at Harrow Street for dinner service only, Thursday through Saturday, up to
60 days in advance through the website or by phone.
- Breakfast, lunch, and Sunday brunch are walk-in only at both locations. No exceptions are made for
parties under 8.
- Parties of 8 to 24 may book The Cellar Room under the private event terms in section 7.
- A reserved table is held for 15 minutes past the reservation time before it is released.
- Parties of 6 or more have an automatic 20 percent gratuity added to the check.
- Reservations of 6 or more that no-show without 4 hours notice are charged $25 per seat to the card on
file.
- The patio is first come first served and cannot be reserved.
9.2 Allergens and Dietary Accommodation
 - The kitchen is not an allergen-free facility. Gluten, dairy, egg, tree nuts, peanuts, soy, fish, and shellfish
are all present and cross-contact is possible.
- Guests with a severe allergy should tell the server before ordering so the ticket is flagged and the item
is fired on dedicated equipment where possible.
- The gluten-free bread swap is prepared on a separate board with separate tools but is toasted in a
shared oven.
- Every menu item is coded in the POS with its allergen list. Staff must read from the POS rather than
answering from memory.
- Full ingredient decks for all items are kept in a binder at each host stand and in the shared drive folder
Operations/Allergens.

10.1 Sourcing Standards
 - Produce must be grown within 120 miles of Brantley Heights during the growing season, from April
through October. Winter sourcing may extend to 400 miles.
- All beef and pork is pasture raised with no routine antibiotics. Bellamy Meats is audited annually by the
executive chef.
- Seafood must carry a green or yellow rating from the Monterey Bay Seafood Watch list. Red-rated
species are never purchased.
- Green coffee is bought at a minimum of $3.10 per pound FOB, well above the commodity floor, and the
price paid to the producer is printed on every retail bag.
- Any vendor with two quality rejections in a rolling quarter goes on review with the executive chef and
may be replaced.

11.2 Delivery and Off-Premise
 - Delivery is offered through two marketplace partners at Harrow Street and one at Riverbend.
Marketplace commission runs 18 to 27 percent depending on the tier.

- Direct online pickup orders through the Copperleaf website carry no markup and no fee.
- Delivery is cut off 45 minutes before kitchen close at both locations.
- Off-premise, meaning delivery plus pickup plus catering, was 29 percent of FY2025 revenue.

event book. February is consistently the weakest month, running about 22 percent below the annual
monthly average. The business holds a cash reserve equal to 90 days of operating expenses,
approximately $890,000, to cover the winter trough and equipment failure.

15. Frequently Asked Questions
What time do you open on Sunday?
Both cafes open at 8:00 AM on Sunday. Harrow Street closes at 3:00 PM and serves brunch only.
Riverbend closes at 4:00 PM.
Are you open on Mondays?
Harrow Street is open every Monday from 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM. Riverbend is closed every Monday.
Do you take reservations?
Only for dinner at Harrow Street, Thursday through Saturday. Everything else is walk-in. Parties of 8 or
more should book The Cellar Room as a private event.
Can I book a private event at Riverbend?
No. Riverbend has no private room and cannot host events. All private events are held in The Cellar Room
at Harrow Street.
How late can I order breakfast?
Until 11:30 AM Monday through Friday, and until 1:00 PM on Saturday and Sunday. The Copperleaf
Biscuit is the only breakfast item available all day.
Do you serve dinner every night?
No. Dinner is served Thursday, Friday, and Saturday from 5:00 PM, at Harrow Street only.
Is there parking?
Harrow Street has 18 dedicated spaces behind the building plus metered street parking. Riverbend shares
a 40 space lot with Kestrel Lane Books.
Do you have gluten-free options?
Yes, including a gluten-free bread swap for $2.00 and a gluten-free olive oil cake. The kitchen is not an
allergen-free facility and cross-contact is possible.
Can I bring my own cake?
Yes, for a $3.50 per guest cutting and plating fee.
How far in advance do I need to order catering?
It depends on the package. Boxed Lunch needs 48 hours, Morning Table needs 72 hours, family style
needs 5 to 7 business days, and Full Service Dinner needs 21 days.
What is your cancellation policy for events?
Full deposit refund less a $75 fee if you cancel 30 or more days out, 50 percent back at 15 to 29 days, no
refund at 8 to 14 days, and the full estimated total is charged inside 7 days.
Do you add gratuity automatically?
Yes for parties of 6 or more, at 20 percent. Catering and private events carry a 20 percent service charge.
Can I bring my dog?
Dogs are welcome on the Harrow Street patio and at the Riverbend benches, but not inside. Service
animals are welcome anywhere.

Do you sell your coffee beans?
Yes, at both cafes and online. Retail bags run $17 to $24 for 12 ounces, and there are four subscription
tiers starting at $16 per shipment.
Can I tour the roastery?
The roastery is closed to the public except for the free Saturday cupping from 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM, which
is walk-in and limited to 14 people per session.
Do gift cards expire?
No. Copperleaf gift cards never expire and carry no fees.
Is there Wi-Fi?
Yes, free. The network is Copperleaf-Guest and the password is on the table cards. Laptop use is asked to
end by 11:30 AM on weekends.
Do you do wholesale bread?
Yes. Bakery wholesale was $118,000 in FY2025. Contact the head baker, Frances Oduya, for current
pricing and delivery days.
