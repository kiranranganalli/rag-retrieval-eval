Acme Corp reported Q3 revenue of $42 million, up 18% year over year.
The company cited strong demand in its cloud infrastructure segment.
Operating margin improved to 22%, driven by cost discipline in engineering.
Management flagged supply chain risk tied to semiconductor shortages as an ongoing concern.
The board approved a $10 million share buyback program effective next quarter.
Customer churn declined to 4.2%, the lowest level in six quarters.
